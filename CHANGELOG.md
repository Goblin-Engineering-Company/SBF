# SBF 2026.09.30.4

**SBF goes Forever.**

The goblins heard Blizzard was reopening the old world, so they packed a tackle box, a crate of explosives, and a very long pole, and got there before the paint dried. SBF now runs on World of Warcraft: Forever. Same one button, same lazy fishing, older rules.

**Your pole goes where Forever wants it.**

In Forever a fishing pole is a two-handed weapon that lives in your main hand, and there is no profession tool slot. SBF knows. Drop your pole into the Fishing pole box on the Profile page, press your fishing key, and that pole goes in. Stop fishing and your real weapon and off-hand come back, no questions asked. Equipment sets work too: SBF slides the pole on first, then finishes your fishing set on the next press, so nothing gets dropped halfway through a swap.

**Catches count as catches.**

Forever uses the classic Fishing spell and SBF recognizes it. Every fish lands in your log and stats as a proper catch, not a mystery crate. Your fishing skill shows up at the top of the window and on the Skill Book tab again, which now lists your one Fishing skill the way Forever tracks it.

**Bobber reach: we fail fast.**

Here is the honest part. In Forever, some casts land farther out than your interact key can grab. While you fish, SBF stretches your interact key's reach, and it can nudge your camera out a little if you're zoomed in close (you can turn that off). Both go back to normal when you stop. Past that, our engineers spent a regrettable amount of blasting powder on cleverer tricks, and every one was a little less reliable than the last. So we stopped fighting the game and started working with it.

SBF fails fast. When a bobber lands out of reach, SBF notices within about a second, plays an optional alert sound, and turns your very next press into a fresh cast. No staring at a bobber you can't reel. No waiting out a dead cast. Press, recast, keep fishing.

For now, about 3 casts in 10 land out of reach on average. That number will move as we learn more, and we'll tell you when it does. Quick tip: the interact key can only grab a bobber your camera can see, so look out over the water rather than at your boots. Find it all under Settings, Bobber reach.

**Stats that play fair.**

Out-of-reach casts get their own line on the Stats tab: how many there were, and what share of your casts they make up. They never count against your catch rate. A cast that never had a chance shouldn't make you look bad in front of the other goblins.

**A new World tab, starting with Patiently Rewarded.**

The Patiently Rewarded alert packed up and moved to its own home: the new World tab. When the buff lands, your screen edges glow gold, SBF tells you a chest has spawned, and your chosen sound plays. It's on by default; switch it off on the World tab any time. Pick the glow color and a sound (the sound stays off until you choose one), and hit Test to see and hear it before it counts. The World tab is where expansion, season, and zone watches will live from now on. Patiently Rewarded just got there first.

**Count your Venom.**

Fishing with The Coiled Huntress? Every catch on the Coiled Isle siphons Venom into the pole, and SBF now shows your total right in the window header, next to your skill and perception, in a suitably poisonous green. Type /sbf venom for the count, what you've gained this session, and your Coiled Filament.

**Your pole, your call.**

The pole in your profile's Fishing pole box is the only pole SBF ever puts on, and a gear set never sneaks a different one in. Leave the box empty and SBF fills it for you: first with the pole saved in your profile's gear set, and on retail with the pole in your fishing tool slot. Clear the box yourself and it stays clear. On Forever, if there's no pole to be found, SBF tells you once and leaves your hands alone. Got two poles? Make a profile for each.

**Punching without a punch button.**

Forever has no Single-Button Assistant, so SBF's default combat action falls back to plain auto-attack. When something decides you look like lunch, your key still swings back. Drag in your own macro any time for real abilities.

**The Equipment Manager button works in Forever.**

One click opens your character sheet straight to the Equipment Manager tab, ready to edit your fishing set.

**Gear sets with a missing piece no longer stall.**

Sold your lucky hat to a vendor? Left your boots in the bank? SBF used to keep trying to put the whole set on and never got around to fishing. Now it equips everything you still have, tells you once which set is short, and gets on with it. Fixed on retail and Forever alike. The hat is still gone, though. We checked.
